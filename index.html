Why we collect

New place. New job. New person.

We're capturing the full range of physical work humans do. That takes real variety, so every recording should bring something new: a different workplace, a different job, a different pair of hands.

The capture cap

One cap. A full working day.

A small camera clips under the front of the brim and records what the wearer sees: their hands, their tools, the work in front of them. No phones. No extra rigs. Just the cap.

What we record

Real work, at real speed.

The best footage is someone doing the job they know, the way they always do it.

We record

  • Real people doing their real job, at normal speed
  • The whole task: setup, the work, the problems, the cleanup
  • Skilled trades with real tools and materials
  • Unusual jobs nobody else is capturing
  • Hands in frame, start to finish

We don't look for

  • Anything staged or acted for the camera
  • Homes. We never record where people live
  • Idle time, waiting around or phone use
  • Objects handed across a table to handle
  • The same simple task, over and over

How it works

A normal day, with a camera cap.

  1. 01

    We meet you at work

    Our team visits your workplace and explains everything before anything starts.

  2. 02

    You say yes

    You give your consent on our app. No consent, no recording.

  3. 03

    You work as normal

    Wear the cap and do your job exactly the way you usually do.

  4. 04

    We collect the cap

    At the end of the day, our team picks the cap back up.

Our promises

People first. Always.

Behind every recording is a person doing a job they know by heart. This is how we treat them, and the places they work.

  • No consent, no recording

    Nothing records until you've said yes on our app.

  • Work your way

    Do your job exactly as you normally do. No acting, no scripts.

  • Never in homes

    We record at workplaces only. Never where people live.

  • Your workplace, your rules

    We follow each site's own rules. A no-filming sign means no filming.

  • Sensitive places stay off-limits

    Clinics, places of worship, schools, banks, government sites and changing areas, unless cleared in advance.

  • Say stop, we stop

    If you, or anyone on site, asks us to stop, we stop.
